# Written Papers

Selected critical disability studies writing by **Eqraa Khan**, written for *CSE241: Introduction to Critical Disability Studies* at the University of Toronto (Computer Science Specialist; Minor in Critical Studies in Equity and Solidarity).

These papers examine how states, institutions, and communities produce and manage disability, drawing on South Asian history, immigration, and policy. Each is a PDF you can open directly.

## Papers

| Paper | Type | Year | Focus |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Starved Into Disability: Colonial Violence, the 1943 Bengal Famine, and the Strategic Creation of a Disabled Bengal](Starved-Into-Disability-1943-Bengal-Famine.pdf) | Research paper | 2026 | Reframes the famine as a disability event, linking Northern and Southern disability studies |
| [She Is Modest. He Is Focused. She Is Easy: How South Asian Families Learn to Story Autism Away](She-Is-Modest-He-Is-Focused-She-Is-Easy.pdf) | Essay | 2026 | Autism, race, immigration, and neoliberal debility in South Asian families |
| [When Disability Becomes Inheritance: Rethinking Bangladesh's Freedom Fighter Quota System](Disability-as-Inheritance-Bangladesh-Quota-OpEd.pdf) | Op-ed | 2025 | Disability, national memory, and the 2024 quota protests in Bangladesh |
